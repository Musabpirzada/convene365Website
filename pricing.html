FAQ

Questions we hear most

How and when do we pay?

Once a year, in advance. Your first invoice covers the setup fee and the first year's subscription; after that you receive one renewal invoice a year. You can pay by bank transfer or by card through a secure invoice link, and we can work with your purchase order process.

Can we pay monthly?

Convene365 is billed yearly. If your organisation needs a different payment schedule, tell us when you contact us and we will look at it together.

What happens to our data if we stop?

It stays where it always was: in your own Dataverse. Nothing is held by us, so you never lose access to your events, attendees or payment records.

Are Microsoft licences included?

No. Power Apps, Power Automate, Power Pages and Power BI licences are bought from Microsoft or your Microsoft partner. We help you work out which ones you need, and many organisations already have some of them.

Do we need Dynamics 365?

No. Convene365 runs on Power Platform with Dataverse. If you do use Dynamics 365, attendees and organisations are the same Contacts and Accounts your sales team already uses.

Where is our data stored?

In your own Dataverse environment, in your Microsoft tenant and region. Stripe only sees the amount and a booking reference, and card details never reach your systems.

How long does an installation take?

About one working day for the install itself, plus waiting time for Microsoft licences. We follow a step-by-step installation guide and finish with a test booking, payment and check-in.

Can attendees pay locally, for example with HyperPay, PayTabs or JazzCash?

Card payments use Stripe today. Local gateways are on the roadmap, and the payment design lets a new gateway be added without changing the rest of the solution.

Does check-in work without internet?

Yes. The Check-in app can work offline from the data it last downloaded and saves check-ins until the connection returns. We recommend one device per entrance while offline.

Can we change it to fit our process?

Yes. Convene365 lives in your Dataverse, so forms, views, approvals and flows can be extended with standard Power Platform tools, and upgrades keep your data.

Can we try it on a real event first?

Yes. We run pilot events: one of your real events on Convene365, with our team alongside you. Ask about a pilot.
