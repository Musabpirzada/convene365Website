Features

Everything an event team needs, on the platform you already trust

From the first plan to the final report, every step happens in your Microsoft environment and every record lands in Dataverse.

Plan

Events, agendas and venues in one app

The Event Lifecycle guides each event through Plan, Build, Publish, Run and Close, showing what is still missing at every stage.

  • In-person, virtual and hybrid events, with the meeting link required where it matters
  • Sessions by day, room and level, kept inside the event's dates automatically
  • Venues and rooms with capacity and layout
  • Event codes, budgets and CPD hours for later certificates
Convene365 Admin › Sessions
Sell

Ticketing that adds up, every time

Several ticket types per event, each with its own price, tax, quantity, sales window and per-order limits. Amounts are calculated on the server, so nobody can change a price in the browser.

Early bird, VIP, student

Any number of ticket types, each sold only inside its own sales window.

Live capacity

Event and ticket limits are checked at the moment of booking, so the last seat sells once.

Card payments

Stripe Checkout, with each payment confirmed with Stripe before the booking is confirmed. Free events confirm instantly.

Register

A branded website your attendees will actually use

Built on Power Pages, in your colours and with your logo, in English.

  • Browse upcoming events, agenda and venue with a map link
  • Book for yourself and colleagues in three short steps
  • Pay by card, or confirm straight away when it's free
  • Ticket email with QR code and calendar file, and a My Tickets page
  • Give feedback after the event: ratings, NPS and comments
events.your-company.com
Check in

Green means go. Red means stop.

The Convene365 Check-in app runs on any phone through Power Apps. Door staff scan the QR code and read the colour.

  • Valid ticket: one tap checks the attendee in
  • Already checked in, cancelled, wrong event or not found: the reason is on screen
  • Search by name or email when there's no QR code
  • Every check-in records the time and who scanned it
  • Works offline in halls with poor signal
Report

Know what every event achieved

Four Power BI pages read your Dataverse directly, so the numbers are always your own. Pick an event from the slicer, or see every event together.

Power BI › Convene365 › Executive overview

Events, registrations, check-in rate, revenue, average rating and NPS at a glance, with revenue by event and a summary table for every event.

Power BI › Convene365 › Registration trend

Tickets booked per day, the mix of ticket types and where bookings came from: the website, the back office or an import.

Power BI › Convene365 › Live event day

Check-ins every 15 minutes, the no-show rate by ticket type and a live list of arrivals, ready for a screen at the venue.

Power BI › Convene365 › Engagement and feedback

Average rating for every session, the spread of NPS answers from 0 to 10, and every comment attendees left.

  • Registrations, check-in rate, revenue, ratings and NPS
  • No-shows by ticket type, and arrivals as they happen
  • Event managers see only their own events

Security

Enterprise controls, without the extra work

Six ready-made roles

Administrator, Event Manager, Coordinator, Finance, Check-in Staff and Executive.

Finance-only amounts

Prices, totals, payments and budgets are visible only to the finance group.

No card data stored

Payment happens on Stripe's secure page. Your systems never see a card number.

Audited check-ins

Every check-in records who scanned it and when; Dataverse auditing logs every change.

Private QR codes

A QR code holds only a random token, never a name or email address.

Your environment

Installed as a managed solution in your own tenant, so your Microsoft policies apply.
