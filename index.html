The problem

Event teams juggle five tools and still can't see the whole picture

Registrations in one place, payments in another, check-in on paper and feedback nowhere. Attendee data ends up outside your tenant, and nobody can say what an event achieved.

Spreadsheets and forms

Bookings live in Excel and Microsoft Forms and are copied by hand.

Data outside your tenant

Ticketing websites keep your attendee data in someone else's cloud.

Overselling

Two people buy the last seat, and someone is turned away at the door.

Paper check-in

Printed lists, long queues and no record of who actually came.

No proof of results

No reliable numbers on attendance, revenue or what attendees thought.

The solution

One solution for the whole event

Convene365 installs into your own Power Platform environment as a single managed solution. Attendees are standard Contacts and organisations are Accounts, ready for Dynamics 365 Sales and Marketing.

  4. Check in

    QR scan at the door, even offline

  5. Report

    Power BI, ratings and NPS

A check-in app that door staff can't get wrong

Door staff pick today's event, scan the QR code and get a clear green or red answer. No code? Search by name.

  • Already-used, cancelled and wrong-event tickets are caught
  • Two doors, one ticket: only the first check-in counts
  • Live arrivals count, and it keeps working without signal
No overselling

The last seat can only be sold once

Capacity is checked inside the booking itself, on the server, not from a counter that refreshes later. When the event fills, it is marked Sold Out and the event manager is emailed.

8people booking the same last seat at once
1booking accepted. The other seven see "Sold out"
  • Event capacity and ticket-type limits both apply
  • Prices are calculated on the server, never in the browser
  • Card payments are confirmed with Stripe before a booking is

Tested live: 8 parallel bookings for a one-seat event, 4 rounds, exactly 1 accepted each round.
